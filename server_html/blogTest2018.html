year 2018 test 1

In android, an activity object may be paused, onStart, onStop many times in the UI layer, but the object created by the activity, especially non UI thread/handler objects, may continue running no matter what is the status of its related activity object.

year 2018 test 2

When software, hardware, firmware are developed parallel, individual team may perform formal verification test separately. There is common risk that the later team discovers bug/issue on other components. For example, when firmware does FW test with mobile application, a mobile app bug is discovered even if the mobile team has finished their verification test and locked source code. Then rework and retest may be forced on the mobile application.

year 2018 test 3

BLE communication has huge asynchronous calls with ‘arbitrary’ timing and sequence. When doing architecture design, the asynchronization, timeout, and retry all need to be considered carefully.
